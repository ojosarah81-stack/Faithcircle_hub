# 💙 FaithCircle
### Grow in Faith. Connect for Real.

Welcome to FaithCircle, an online community designed to help Christian young adults connect, learn, fellowship, and grow spiritually together.

## 🌍 About FaithCircle

FaithCircle is an independent Christian community platform for young adults aged 18–30, welcoming believers from different churches and denominations.

Our goal is to create a welcoming digital space where young Christians can build meaningful friendships, study the Bible, encourage one another, and strengthen their relationship with God.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To connect Christian young adults through faith, fellowship, biblical learning, and meaningful relationships.

## ✨ Features

FaithCircle aims to provide:

- 📖 **Bible Study:** Explore Scripture, join Bible study groups, and learn together.
- 🧠 **Bible Quizzes:** Test your Bible knowledge and earn points.
- 💬 **Community Discussions:** Share ideas, ask questions, and learn from other believers.
- 🙏 **Prayer Room:** Share prayer requests and encourage one another.
- 🤝 **Mentorship:** Connect with suitable mentors and accountability partners.
- 🏆 **Points and Rewards:** Earn points, unlock badges, progress through levels, and participate in friendly competitions.
- 📚 **Bible Reading Plans:** Build consistency in studying God's Word.
- 🎥 **Virtual Fellowship:** Participate in online gatherings, voice calls, and video fellowship as these features are developed.

## 👥 Who Is FaithCircle For?

FaithCircle is designed for Christian young adults aged 18–30 who want to:

- Grow in their relationship with God.
- Understand the Bible better.
- Make meaningful Christian friendships.
- Share experiences and encourage others.
- Participate in Bible studies and Christian activities.
- Develop consistency in their spiritual growth.

## 🎨 Our Brand

- **Name:** FaithCircle
- **Tagline:** Grow in Faith. Connect for Real.
- **Primary Colours:** Blue and white
- **Audience:** Christian young adults aged 18–30
- **Platform:** Website first, with the possibility of a mobile app in the future.

## 🚀 Project Status

FaithCircle is currently in its early development stage.

The initial prototype demonstrates the website's visual direction and selected interactive features. Real user accounts, persistent data storage, live messaging, voice calls, and video fellowship still need to be implemented.

## 🛡️ Community Values

FaithCircle is committed to building a community founded on:

- Love and respect.
- Biblical learning.
- Encouragement and accountability.
- Respectful conversations across Christian denominations.
- Privacy and responsible community participation.
- A safe and welcoming environment.

We envision FaithCircle becoming a trusted digital community where Christian young adults worldwide can connect, learn, fellowship, and grow together in Christ.
